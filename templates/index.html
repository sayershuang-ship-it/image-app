How It Works

Three steps from idea to image

No prompt engineering required. Drop in a reference, describe what you want, and let the model do the heavy lifting.

01

Upload a reference

Drop a photo, sketch, or style reference. GPT-4o Vision analyzes it and folds the visual context into your prompt automatically.

02

Describe your vision

Write what you want in plain language. Hit "Translate & Enhance" and GPT-4o rewrites it into a detailed, model-optimized prompt.

03

Generate & refine

Choose quality, size, and count. Generate multiple variations at once. Download, copy prompts, and iterate from history.

Features

Built for people who generate a lot of images

Not a toy. Real controls, persistent history, and tooling for iteration at speed.

Context-aware

Reference image upload

Drop a JPEG, PNG, or WebP and GPT-4o Vision reads the style, composition, and palette before enhancing your prompt. No more guessing at style tokens.

Prompt engineering, automated

GPT-4o prompt translation

Write casually. One click runs your prompt through GPT-4o, which expands it into the kind of detailed, structured description that GPT Image 2 responds to best.

Full control

Quality, size & count

Low, medium, or high quality. Sizes from 1024×1024 up to 2048×2048. Generate 1–4 variations per run. Every parameter is exposed.

Never lose a good prompt

Persistent prompt memory

Every generation is saved to SQLite with the original prompt, revised prompt, parameters, and result. Search, restore, and iterate from anything in your history.
